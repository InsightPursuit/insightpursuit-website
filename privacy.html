Privacy

How we handle your information

This notice covers insightpursuit.com. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms:

  • Waitlist: your email address.
  • Contact form for companies: your work email and, if you add them, your name, your organisation and what you need to keep track of.
  • Early-access form on our About page: your email address, the option you choose and, if you add them, your name, interest, game or topic, how you heard about us and a short note.

With any form we also record the website that linked you here and any campaign tags in the link (such as utm_source), so we know which channels work.

How we use it

To reply to you, and to contact you about Insight Pursuit and early access to our products. We don't sell your details or share them for anyone else's marketing.

Where it is kept

Form submissions are stored by Netlify, the company that hosts this website, and are only visible to us.

What this site does not do

  • No advertising or tracking cookies.
  • No analytics scripts.

The site loads its typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser requests them from Google's servers. The GTA VI trailer is hosted by YouTube. Its preview image loads from YouTube's image server. The video itself only loads if you press play, and it uses YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode. Once it plays, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the details you sent. Just reply to any email we send you.
