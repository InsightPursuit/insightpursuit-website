How we work

Four steps, every time.

  1. 01

    Collect

    We gather every claim on a topic, with where it came from and when.

  2. 02

    Grade

    Each claim gets a clear label for how solid it is, with the sources linked.

  3. 03

    Track

    We watch for changes. When a claim moves, the label changes, and the old version stays on record.

  4. 04

    Report

    You get a short, dated brief: what's confirmed, what isn't, and what changed since last time.

How we grade

Every claim gets a label you can check.

We never blur what's official with what's rumoured. Each label says what a claim rests on.

Official
Stated by the company or organisation itself, on its own channels.
Seen in official material
Visible or audible in official material, such as a trailer or preview, but not stated in writing.
Reported
Stated by a news outlet, without an official source we could check.
Unverified
Rests only on a leak, a deleted post or a single unchecked source.
